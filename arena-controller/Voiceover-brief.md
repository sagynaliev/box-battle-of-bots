# Box Battle of Bots — Arena announcer

Record eight English lines for a live robot combat arena. Use a clear, confident sports-announcer delivery. Leave a short beat between the countdown numbers and punch “FIGHT.” Deliver “Stop the battle” firmly and clearly. Give both team introductions and both winner announcements the same energy.

Deliver one clean WAV per line, preferably 48 kHz/24-bit. Use a consistent level throughout, with clean peaks and no clipping. Leave out music, reverb, and crowd effects.
